FAQ

Before you sign.

Can I visit before choosing?

Yes. Book a visit online and we will show you the three floors and La Verrière.

How do I book a meeting room?

From your member area. You see live availability, choose your slot and receive the confirmation by email.

Where can I find my invoices?

In your member area, with your contract and your booking history.

Can I change membership?

Yes. Talk to the team on site or write to us, and we will adjust your membership to suit you.
